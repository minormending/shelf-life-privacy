Shelf Life privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Shelf Life is a cozy wine-shop game, by Standard Mending Co. (Kevin Ramdath). This policy covers the Shelf Life app on Android. Shelf Life is intended for adults of legal drinking age. It sells nothing and links to no shop.

What we collect

Almost nothing. Shelf Life has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your name, contacts, location or anything you do in the game, and we do not sell or share personal information. The only thing that leaves your device is the update check described below.

What stays on your device

Your grades, stars, tips, Daily Pick results, streak, Wine Book and settings are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them. When you share a Daily Pick result, the app hands the text to your phone's share sheet; you choose where it goes.

Game updates

When the app starts, it checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for a newer version of the game's content. That request carries technical details needed to deliver the update, such as the app version, platform and update channel, plus a random install identifier that the update service creates the first time the app runs. The identifier is not linked to you or your device's hardware; it lets the service roll updates out gradually and count how many installs run each version. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. The request does not include your name, contacts, location or anything you do in the game. Expo's own privacy policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

Children

Shelf Life is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
